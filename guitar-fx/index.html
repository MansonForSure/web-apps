Setup guide

01 · CONNECT

Get a clean signal in

For an electric guitar, use a guitar audio interface (USB-C or Lightning) and plug headphones into it or into the phone. The built-in microphone works for acoustic guitar and quick tries. If your interface has two inputs, pick the input your guitar is plugged into under Channel.

02 · GAIN STAGE

Set Input, then Master

Play hard and raise Input until the meter peaks around −12 dB without CLIP. Then set Master to a comfortable listening level. A limiter at the output keeps sudden peaks below 0 dB.

03 · UNDER THE HOOD

Where the GPU helps

Effects run on the browser’s real-time audio thread with native filters, convolution and AudioWorklet code. That is the lowest-latency path. Sending 2.7 ms audio blocks to the GPU and back would add more delay than it saves. The GPU draws the live spectrum instead, which keeps the page smooth.
