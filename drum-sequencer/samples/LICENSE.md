# Drum sample licence

The audio files in this folder are derived from **Naked Drums** by Wilkinson Audio
(© Wilkinson Audio 2016), in the SFZ edition by kinwie published by the SFZ Instruments
project: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/WilkinsonAudio.NakedDrums

They are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** licence
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ — full text in `CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

Changes made for Backbeat: each stroke was mixed down from its original multi-microphone
FLAC recordings to a single stereo file, following the library's default stereo preset
levels, pans and trims (with the far-room mics 2 dB and the mid-side pair 1 dB lower). The files were then
level-normalised, trimmed of leading silence and inaudible tails, faded out, reduced to a
subset of velocity layers and round robins, and encoded as MP3. The scripts used are in
`../tools/`.
