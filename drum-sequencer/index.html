Why it sounds real

Every hit is a recording of a real drum. The kit is a Yamaha Recording Custom with a 22″ kick, 10–16″ toms, two snares, and Sabian and Zildjian cymbals. It was recorded through Neve and API preamps, with close mics, overheads, close and far room mics, and a mid-side pair.

For this app each hit was mixed from all of those microphones into stereo. The mix follows the library's own default balance and uses the drummer's perspective, so the hi-hat is on the left and the floor tom is on the right.

The dynamics you paint choose between recorded strokes, from soft ghost notes to full accents, so a quiet hit also has a softer tone. Repeated hits rotate through different takes, which avoids the "machine-gun" sound. Humanize adds small timing and dynamics changes like a real drummer. Closing the hi-hat cuts off an open hat.
