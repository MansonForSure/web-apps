A QUICK TOUR

Just put your fingers down.

Press the letter and number keys shown in the layout. R is C4, 5 is C♯4, and E is B3. The 4 key is silent, as are 1, 7 and minus. Hold several keys for a chord.

On a touchscreen, play the piano or the shortcut buttons with multiple fingers. Slide a finger across the piano for a glissando. On a small screen, swipe the strip beneath the keys to explore the range.

Transpose shifts the sound by up to 12 semitones in either direction. The piano highlights the actual pitch; the computer-key layout keeps your fingering the same. Changing transpose releases held notes.

Hold SPACE for sustain, or tap the Sustain toggle. Press ESC for all notes off. Key labels can be hidden. The first key or Enable sound unlocks browser audio.

Synthesized piano sound, made locally in your browser. No microphone, no account, no uploaded audio. Some physical keyboards limit how many keys can register together.
